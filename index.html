What's inside, day by day

Your simple starting point for 5 days

Five short, repeatable days — built to calm things down, get things moving again, and help you remember what “normal” feels like.

Day 1 / 5

Wake up gently

Slow mornings, warm foods, easing off anything harsh on a sensitive gut.

Today isn't about restriction — it's about removing extra work for your gut.
Day 2 / 5

Add some movement

Gentle fibre, a short walk, and easing constipation the natural way.

A 10–15 minute walk after meals is one of the simplest tools there is.
Day 3 / 5

Feed the good bacteria

Introducing fermented foods slowly — think a spoonful, not a bowlful.

New to fermented foods? Start small and see how you feel.
Day 4 / 5

Steady & satisfied

Balanced plates and protein at every meal, with gentle consistency.

By now, notice: less bloated by evening? Sleeping better? Write it down.
Day 5 / 5

Notice & reflect

A calm close to the reset, and a look back at what you learned.

This is a rhythm to return to, not a five-day sprint.
